## 2. ESCCOM: National Postcode and Addressing Project

**To:** [ESCCOM email]
**Subject:** Developer tools for the National Postcode and Addressing Project (Matsapha rollout)

Dear ESCCOM team,

I am [Name], a software developer in Eswatini. I have followed the National Postcode and Addressing Project and
the Matsapha rollout with interest. I am building **Ekhaya**, a set of developer tools (an API, a mobile app and an
SDK) so that couriers, emergency services and businesses can use the official addresses in their own systems. It
follows the SZNS ISO 19160 structure and does not create a separate address format.

To make sure Ekhaya supports the national project and does not duplicate or conflict with it, I would appreciate
guidance on the following:

1. **Data access.** Will the national address database be available to developers, for example through an API or
   periodic export? What are the access terms?
2. **Address identifiers.** Will each address receive an official identifier or code, and in what format?
3. **Matsapha rollout.** Which streets and plots have signs and addresses assigned, and when will the list be
   published?
4. **Pilot partners.** Could you introduce me to licensed couriers who might pilot the tools? I would also welcome
   any feedback from ESCCOM on the approach.
5. **Standard text.** Is the Eswatini profile of ISO 19160 available to developers other than through ESWASA?

I would be glad to meet, in person or by call, and to show you a working prototype once it is ready. My aim is to
complement the national project, and I will follow any data-protection and usage rules you set.

Kind regards,
[Name]
